## 5. Jobs, offers, and marketplace rules

### 5.1 Posting and browsing jobs

Customers may post job requests describing work they want done, including relevant details such as category, description, location information, and photos where supported.

Professionals may browse open jobs and decide whether to submit an offer.

Customers remain responsible for the accuracy and lawfulness of their job posts.

### 5.2 Offers

Professionals who have enabled professional functionality may submit offers on open jobs, subject to in-app rules.

- Professionals may submit **unlimited offers** over time.
- A Professional may submit **only one offer per job**.
- Professionals may not submit an offer on their own job.

Offer prices and messages are set by the Professional. They are displayed to help users communicate and decide whether to work together. They do **not** create a payment obligation processed by SOLVEQO for the physical service (see Section 7).

### 5.3 Accepting offers and SOLVEQO Pro eligibility

Customers decide whether to accept an offer.

For each Professional:

- the **first three (3) successfully accepted jobs** in that Professional’s account history are available **without** an active SOLVEQO Pro subscription;
- after a Professional has **three (3) lifetime accepted jobs**, an active **SOLVEQO Pro** subscription is required before **another offer from that same Professional can be accepted**;
- only **accepted** jobs count toward this limit. Pending, rejected, declined, withdrawn, expired, or otherwise non-accepted offers do **not** count as accepted jobs.

This rule affects whether a Customer can accept a Professional’s offer in the app. It is **not** a commission, transaction fee, or charge on the payment for physical work between users.

### 5.4 Job status and cancellation

Jobs move through statuses shown in the app, such as open, assigned, in progress, completed, or cancelled, depending on user actions and platform rules.

Customers may cancel job requests only where the app allows it and according to the job’s current state. For example, cancellation may not be available once offers are pending or accepted.

Professionals and Customers must cooperate in good faith when updating job status, communicating, and completing work through available in-app flows.
